
Mobile & On-body systems = Behaviour, Exposure, Personalised prevention, cure and care.

Mobile and on-body systems are technologies such as smartphones, smartwatches, fitness trackers, and wearable medical sensors that collect health-related information to help monitor, prevent, and treat diseases.

- Behaviour → What you do.
- Exposure → What affects you from your surroundings.
- Prevention → How to avoid diseases.
- Cure → How to treat diseases.
- Care → How to monitor and support health.
