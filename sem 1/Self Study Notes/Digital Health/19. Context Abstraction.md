
---
## 1. Context Representation in Time
Sequential temporal tracking of discrete activity states:

`Entering` $\rightarrow$ `Greeting` $\rightarrow$ `PC work` $\rightarrow$ `Leaving` $\rightarrow$ `Riding train`

---

## 2. Context Type Representation

Multimodal domain categories used to define user situation:

* **User Activity:** Physical movements, routines, and actions (e.g., walking, working, eating).
* **User State:** Internal physiological or cognitive states (e.g., stress, heart rate, fatigue).
* **Environmental State:** Surrounding ambient conditions (e.g., light, noise level, temperature).
* **Extended Location:** Spatial context beyond raw GPS (e.g., office, home, transit vehicle).

---

## 3. Modes of Utilising Context Information

* **Active:** System automatically alters its behavior, features, or responses based on detected context changes.
* **Passive:** System presents updated context to the user or logs it for long-term tracking and review.
