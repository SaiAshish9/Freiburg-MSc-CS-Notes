
- **Medical Datum:** Any single observation of a patient, defined by four core elements:
    1. **Patient in question**
    2. **Parameter being observed**
    3. **Value of the parameter**
    4. **Time of observation**        
        _(A datum may have an associated uncertainty; sampling strategies apply.)_
        
- **Medical Data:** A collection of individual observations.


A **medical datum** (plural: _medical data_) is the most fundamental unit of clinical information. It represents a single, atomic observation recorded about a patient during medical care or research.

### Types of Medical Data
- **Narrative, Text, & Coding:** Diagnoses, clinical conditions, progress notes, medical codes, etc.
- **Numerical Measurements:** Laboratory tests (e.g., blood tests), vital signs, and quantitative metrics.
- **Recorded Signals:** Time-series data (e.g., electrocardiogram / ECG).
- **Images:** Radiographic images (e.g., X-rays, CT/MRI scans), anatomical sketches, and visual recordings.
