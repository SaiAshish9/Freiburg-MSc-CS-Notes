Digital Health is the use of digital technologies, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and data science to improve healthcare, medical diagnosis, treatment, and patient monitoring.

### Main topics in Digital Health

1. Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare – Using machine learning and deep learning to predict diseases and assist medical diagnosis.
2. Medical Image Analysis – Analyzing X-rays, MRI scans, and CT scans using computer vision.
3. Electronic Health Records (EHR) – Storing and analyzing patients' medical information.
4. Wearable Devices – Monitoring heart rate, sleep, and physical activity using smartwatches and sensors.
5. Healthcare Data Analytics – Discovering patterns in medical datasets.
6. Telemedicine – Providing healthcare consultations remotely.
7. Privacy and Security – Protecting sensitive medical information.
