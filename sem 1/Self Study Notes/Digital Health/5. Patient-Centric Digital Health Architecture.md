
The surrounding outer nodes represent key entity domains that bi-directionally exchange data with the personal sensor network:

- **Practitioner:** Medical doctors/clinicians who review longitudinal patient data, issue clinical decisions, and remotely monitor patient conditions.

- **Call Centre / Assistance:** Telehealth, emergency services, and automated assistance networks providing real-time triaging or emergency alerts.

- **Device Manufacturer:** Providers responsible for firmware/hardware updates, algorithm calibrations, and device security compliance.

- **Medical Center (Hospital):** Healthcare facilities receiving acute health alerts, diagnostic uploads, or continuous Electronic Health Record (EHR) syncs.

- **Insurance:** Payers and health management networks evaluating preventive measures, digital therapeutics, and outcomes-based care.

- **Therapy:** Physical therapists, rehabilitation specialists, or behavioral health providers who customize and monitor recovery programs based on sensor feedback.
