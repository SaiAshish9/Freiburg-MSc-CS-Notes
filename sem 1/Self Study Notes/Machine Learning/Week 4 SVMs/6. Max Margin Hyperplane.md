## Hard-Margin SVM: Scale Fixing & Linear Separability

### 1. Simplifying the Optimization (Fixing Scale Invariance)

A separating hyperplane is defined as:
$$\mathcal{H}(\boldsymbol{w}, w_0) = \{\boldsymbol{x} \mid \boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x} + w_0 = 0\}$$

* **Scale Invariance:** Multiplying both $\boldsymbol{w}$ and $w_0$ by any scalar $c \neq 0$ leaves the hyperplane $\mathcal{H}(\boldsymbol{w}, w_0)$ unchanged, meaning infinitely many parameter pairs $(\boldsymbol{w}, w_0)$ describe the exact same plane.
* **Canonical Normalization:** To uniquely fix the scale, restrict $(\boldsymbol{w}, w_0)$ so that the closest training point(s) have a functional margin of $1$:
  $$\min_{\boldsymbol{x} \in \{\boldsymbol{x}_1, \dots, \boldsymbol{x}_N\}} |\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x} + w_0| = 1$$
* **Geometric Margin Simplification:** The perpendicular distance from any point $\boldsymbol{x}$ to $\mathcal{H}$ is $\frac{|\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x} + w_0|}{\|\boldsymbol{w}\|_2}$. Under canonical normalization, the margin simplifies to $\frac{1}{\|\boldsymbol{w}\|_2}$.

**Constrained Optimization Formulation:**
$$\begin{aligned}
\arg\max_{\boldsymbol{w}, w_0} \quad & \frac{1}{\|\boldsymbol{w}\|_2} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \forall i:\; y_i (\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0) \ge 0 \\
& \min_{\boldsymbol{x} \in \{\boldsymbol{x}_1, \dots, \boldsymbol{x}_N\}} |\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x} + w_0| = 1
\end{aligned}$$

*(Note: For linearly separable data, tightening the constraint to $y_i(\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0) \ge 1$ automatically enforces the minimum magnitude condition at the optimum, turning the problem into minimizing $\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{w}\|_2^2$.)*

### 2. Violations to the Linear Separability Assumption
Consider the hard-margin quadratic program:
$$\begin{aligned}
\arg\min_{\boldsymbol{w}, w_0} \quad & \boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{w} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \forall i:\; y_i (\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0) \ge 1
\end{aligned}$$

* **Feasibility:** **No solution exists** when the classes are not linearly separable.
* **Why:** The constraint requires every positive point ($y_i = +1$) to satisfy $\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0 \ge 1$ and every negative point ($y_i = -1$) to satisfy $\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0 \le -1$. When class regions overlap (e.g., a $y = -1$ point lies on the positive side of the hyperplane where $\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0 > 0$), $y_i(\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0) < 0 \not\ge 1$, leaving an **empty feasible set**.
* **Resolution (Soft-Margin SVM):** Introduce slack variables $\xi_i \ge 0$ to allow margin violations penalized by regularization parameter $C > 0$:
  $$\begin{aligned}
  \arg\min_{\boldsymbol{w}, w_0, \boldsymbol{\xi}} \quad & \frac{1}{2}\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{w} + C \sum_{i=1}^N \xi_i \\
  \text{s.t.} \quad & \forall i:\; y_i (\boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{x}_i + w_0) \ge 1 - \xi_i, \quad \xi_i \ge 0
  \end{aligned}$$
