## Machine Learning Design Cycle

**Definition:** An iterative process for designing, training, evaluating, and improving ML models.

### Six Stages

1. **Preprocessing:** Collect and clean data; handle missing values, outliers, and standardization.
2. **Feature Extraction:** Convert raw data into meaningful features.
3. **Feature Selection:** Select the most relevant features.
4. **Machine Learning:** Train the prediction model.
5. **Evaluation & Model Selection:** Evaluate performance, tune hyperparameters, and ensure generalization.
6. **Post-Processing:** Refine predictions, calibrate probabilities, and consider fairness.

**Key Takeaway:** The ML design cycle is iterative. Evaluate the model and revisit earlier stages to improve performance on unseen data.
