
Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) is a method for finding the model parameters (theta) that make the observed training data most probable.


**Definition:** Finds model parameters that maximize the likelihood of observed training data.

**Likelihood:**
$$L(\theta)=\prod_{n=1}^{N}\hat p(y_n\mid x_n;\theta)$$

**Log-Likelihood:**
$$\log L(\theta)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\log\hat p(y_n\mid x_n;\theta)$$

**Gaussian Error Assumption:**
$$\epsilon\mid x\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)$$

**Key Result (fixed variance):**
$$\arg\max_\theta\log L(\theta)=\arg\min_\theta\sum_{n=1}^{N}(y_n-\hat y_n)^2$$

**Takeaway:** MLE under Gaussian noise is equivalent to minimizing squared prediction errors.
