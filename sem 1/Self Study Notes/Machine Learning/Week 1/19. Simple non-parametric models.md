## K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

**Definition:** A non-parametric supervised ML algorithm that predicts a target using the k closest training examples.

**Distance:**
$$d(x,z)=\|x-z\|_p$$

**Prediction:**
$$f(x)=\operatorname{aggregate}(\pi_y(\mathcal S(x)))$$

**Regression:** Average the target values.
$$\hat y=\frac{1}{k}\sum_{i=1}^{k}y_i$$

**Classification:** Majority vote.
$$f(x)_c=\frac{\text{Number of neighbors in class }c}{k}$$

- Small K: Sensitive to noise, risk of overfitting.
- Large K: Smoother predictions, risk of underfitting.

**Key takeaway:** Similar data points are assumed to have similar targets.
