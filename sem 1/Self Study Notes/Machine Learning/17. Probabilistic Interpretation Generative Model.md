A generative model describes how data could be generated using a probability distribution, accounting for uncertainty and prediction errors.

Suppose a model predicts a house price of €300,000 with an error standard deviation of €20,000.

 y | x ~ N (300000,20000^2)

This means the predicted price is €300,000, but the actual price may differ due to random variation.
