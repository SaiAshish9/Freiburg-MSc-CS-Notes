### 1. Underfitting (High Bias)

- Model is too simple to capture the underlying patterns.
- Performs poorly on both training and test data.
- Solution: Increase model complexity or use more informative features.

### 2. Overfitting (High Variance)

- Model is too complex and learns noise along with patterns.
- Performs very well on training data but poorly on unseen test data.
- Solution: Apply regularization to improve generalization.

### 3. Regularization

- A technique used to reduce overfitting by discouraging unnecessary model complexity.
- Helps the model generalize better to unseen data.
- Common techniques include L1 and L2 regularization.
