### MLP Forward Pass Complexity (Single Data Point)

- **Input dimensionality:** $d$
- **Hidden layers:** Size $k_1$ and $k_2$
- **Output layer:** Size $c$

#### 1. Time Complexity

- **Layer 1 ($d \to k_1$):** $\mathcal{O}(d \cdot k_1)$
- **Layer 2 ($k_1 \to k_2$):** $\mathcal{O}(k_1 \cdot k_2)$
- **Output Layer ($k_2 \to c$):** $\mathcal{O}(k_2 \cdot c)$
- **Total Time:** $\mathcal{O}(d k_1 + k_1 k_2 + k_2 c)$

#### 2. Memory Complexity

- **Includes:** Storing input features, weights, biases, and intermediate layer activations.
- **Parameters & Intermediates:** $\mathcal{O}(d + k_1 + k_2 + c + dk_1 + k_1k_2 + k_2c)$
