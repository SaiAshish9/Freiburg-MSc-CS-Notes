### Perceptron Forward Pass ($B$ samples, $d$ dimensions)

**1. Pre-activation:**

  

$$Z = XW + b$$

- $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d}$ (Input batch)
- $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times 1}$ (Weights)
- $b \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 1}$ (Bias)
- $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Pre-activation output)  

**2. Activation:**
$$Y = f(Z)$$

- $f(\cdot)$ (Activation function)
- $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Output batch)

### MLP Complexity ($M$ layers, $k$ units/layer, batch $B$, input $d$)

- **Time Complexity:**$$\mathcal{O}(B \cdot d \cdot k + M \cdot B \cdot k^2)$$
    (First layer: $\mathcal{O}(Bdk)$; Remaining $M-1$ layers: $\mathcal{O}(MBk^2)$)

- **Memory Complexity:**$$\mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2 + Bd + BMk)$$
    (Params: $\mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2)$ | Activations: $\mathcal{O}(Bd + BMk)$)
