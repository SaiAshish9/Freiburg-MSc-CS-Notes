### Perceptron Forward Pass ($B$ samples, $d$ dimensions)

**1. Pre-activation:**

  

$$Z = XW + b$$

- $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d}$ (Input batch)
- $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times 1}$ (Weights)
- $b \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 1}$ (Bias)
- $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Pre-activation output)  

**2. Activation:**
$$Y = f(Z)$$

- $f(\cdot)$ (Activation function)
- $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Output batch)

### MLP Complexity ($M$ layers, $k$ units/layer, batch $B$, input $d$)

- **Time Complexity:**$$\mathcal{O}(B \cdot d \cdot k + M \cdot B \cdot k^2)$$
    (First layer: $\mathcal{O}(Bdk)$; Remaining $M-1$ layers: $\mathcal{O}(MBk^2)$)

- **Memory Complexity:**$$\mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2 + Bd + BMk)$$
    (Params: $\mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2)$ | Activations: $\mathcal{O}(Bd + BMk)$)


### 1. Perceptron Forward Pass (Batch Size $B$)

For a batch of $B$ data points with $d$ dimensions:

- **Formula 1 (Linear Transformation / Pre-activation):**
    
    $$Z = XW + b$$
    
    - $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d}$ (Input batch matrix)
        
    - $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times 1}$ (Weight vector)
        
    - $b \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 1}$ (Bias, broadcasted across the batch)
        
    - $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Pre-activation output vector)
        
- **Formula 2 (Activation Function):**
    
    $$Y = f(Z)$$
    
    - $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Pre-activation input)
        
    - $f(\cdot)$ (Activation function, e.g., ReLU, Sigmoid)
        
    - $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1}$ (Final output batch)
        

### 2. MLP Time Complexity ($M$ Layers of $k$ Units, Batch $B$, Input $d$)

- **First Layer ($d \to k$):**
    
    - Multiplies input matrix $(B \times d)$ by weights $(d \times k)$, taking $\mathcal{O}(B \cdot d \cdot k)$.
        
- **Remaining Layers ($M-1$ layers of size $k \to k$):**
    
    - Each layer multiplies an activation matrix $(B \times k)$ by weights $(k \times k)$, taking $\mathcal{O}(B \cdot k^2)$ per layer. Across $M-1$ layers, this is $\mathcal{O}(M \cdot B \cdot k^2)$.
        
- **Total Time Complexity:**
    
    $$\mathcal{O}(B \cdot d \cdot k + M \cdot B \cdot k^2) \quad \text{or} \quad \mathcal{O}(B(dk + Mk^2))$$
    

### 3. MLP Memory Complexity ($M$ Layers of $k$ Units, Batch $B$, Input $d$)

Memory includes both **model parameters** (weights and biases) and **intermediate activations** (stored during the forward pass):

- **Parameters (Weights + Biases):**
    
    - Layer 1: $d \cdot k + k = \mathcal{O}(dk)$
        
    - Subsequent $M-1$ layers: $(M-1)(k^2 + k) = \mathcal{O}(M k^2)$
        
    - _Parameter Total:_ $\mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2)$
        
- **Activations & Inputs (Scaled by Batch Size $B$):**
    
    - Input tensor: $B \times d = \mathcal{O}(B \cdot d)$
        
    - Hidden layer activations: $M$ layers of size $B \times k = \mathcal{O}(B \cdot M \cdot k)$
        
    - _Activation Total:_ $\mathcal{O}(Bd + BMk)$
        
- **Total Memory Complexity:**
    
    $$\mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2 + Bd + BMk) \quad \text{or} \quad \mathcal{O}(dk + Mk^2 + B(d + Mk))$$
