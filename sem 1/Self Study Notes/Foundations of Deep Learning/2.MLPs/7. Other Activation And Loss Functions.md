### . Softmax vs. Hard-Max in Multiclass Classification

- **Differentiability:**
    - Hard-max is non-differentiable (gradients are zero everywhere or undefined), which breaks gradient-based optimization via backpropagation.
    - Softmax is smooth and differentiable, allowing continuous gradient flow.
    
- **Probabilistic Output & Loss:**
    - Softmax converts raw scores (logits) into a strictly positive probability distribution that sums to $1$, enabling cross-entropy loss and model confidence scores.
    - Hard-max outputs hard one-hot vectors, destroying gradient signals regarding _how close_ incorrect classes were to being chosen.

### 2. ReLU Output Activation for Regression

- **Output Range Limitation:**
    - ReLU clips all negative values to $0$ ($\max(0, x)$).
    
- **Consequence:**
    - The model **cannot predict negative values**. If the target regression variable contains negative values (e.g., financial losses, temperatures below zero, net changes), predictions will be severely bounded and biased at zero.
    
- **Gradient Stagnation:**
    - Any samples producing negative pre-activations receive a gradient of $0$, completely halting parameter updates for those paths during training.
