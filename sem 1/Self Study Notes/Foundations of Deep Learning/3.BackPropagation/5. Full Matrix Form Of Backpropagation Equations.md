## 2-Layer MLP Matrix Formulation (Sigmoid Activations)

### Matrix Notation
* **Input:** $\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times D}$
* **Hidden Layer:** 
  $$\mathbf{Z}_0 = \mathbf{X} \mathbf{W}_0 + \mathbf{B}_0$$
  $$\mathbf{A}_0 = \sigma(\mathbf{Z}_0) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\mathbf{Z}_0}}$$
* **Output Layer:** 
  $$\mathbf{Z}_1 = \mathbf{A}_0 \mathbf{W}_1 + \mathbf{B}_1$$
  $$\hat{\mathbf{Y}} = \mathbf{A}_1 = \sigma(\mathbf{Z}_1) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\mathbf{Z}_1}}$$

### Explicit Combined Equation
$$\hat{\mathbf{Y}} = \sigma \left( \sigma \left( \mathbf{X} \mathbf{W}_0 + \mathbf{B}_0 \right) \mathbf{W}_1 + \mathbf{B}_1 \right)$$
