### What Does the Blue Circle Represent?

- The **blue circle** represents a specific **data point or instance** plotted in the feature space.
- Because it falls on the lower-right side of the decision boundary, the trained classification model predicts its class label based on which side of the red line it lands on (in this context, separating _apples_ from _pears_).
