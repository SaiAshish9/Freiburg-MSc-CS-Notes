
Deep learning relies on fast matrix operations 
1. In fact, tensor operations (tensors are like matrices, but have an arbitrary number of dimensions) - Almost all the math is written in matrix/vector form
2. E.g., backpropagation in matrix form is heavy on matrix algebra 
 
Some example concepts we’ll require - 
1. Eigenvectors, eigenvalues, eigendecomposition 
2. Hessians and Jacobians
