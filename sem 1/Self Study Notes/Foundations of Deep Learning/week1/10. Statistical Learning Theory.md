Bias-Variance 
	Analysis to decompose generalization error (shown here for MSE)

expected loss = (bias)^2 + variance + noise

Vapnik-Chervonenkis dimension 
	Bound on the generalization error, typically far too large for deep learning, thus not practically useful

With a growing capacity of your model, the bias goes down, the variance goes up, and that there is sort of the sweet spot in between.
