### **Grammar Box (G) on the Right: Genitiv (Genitive Case)**

- **Genitiv: Name + s** = Genitive: Name + s
- **die Schwester von Julia** = the sister of Julia →\rightarrow→ **Julias Schwester** = Julia's sister
- **! die Freundin von Jonas** = the girlfriend of Jonas →\rightarrow→ **Jonas' Freundin** = Jonas' girlfriend _(Note: nouns ending in -s just take an apostrophe)_
- **! auch nach -ß, -x, -z:** = also after -ß, -x, -z:
    - **Frau Weiß’ Kollegin** = Mrs. Weiß's colleague
    - **Max’ Bruder** = Max's brother
    - **Liz’ Freund** = Liz's friend
